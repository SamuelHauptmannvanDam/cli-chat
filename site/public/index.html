## 01 · three gears

From inbox to autopilot, at your pace.

Three modes. Switch a running inbox with one word.

"chat"

Live inbox — you answer

Your inbox streams into the session. Answer one, some, or all in a line.

say "stop" to close it

❯ chat

· listening…

📨 Niels · #1

did the deploy go out this morning?

📨 Mette · #2

lunch thursday?

❯ 1: yes, at 9 — 2: can't, I have standup

↳ 📤 Sent to Niels — "Yes, went out at 9."

↳ 📤 Sent to Mette — "Can't — I have standup."

"auto draft chat"

Assistant drafts — you send

Every message gets a suggested reply. Nothing sends until you say so.

"draft" flips a running chat into this mode

❯ auto draft chat

📨 Sam · #1

any chance you can review my PR today?

↳ ✏️ draft for Sam: "Yes — after standup, around 11."

📨 Niels · #2

what's the new ingest endpoint called?

↳ ✏️ draft for Niels: "/v2/ingest — merged this morning."

❯ send 2 · 1: make it after lunch

↳ 📤 Sent to Niels — "/v2/ingest — merged this morning."

↳ 📤 Sent to Sam — "Yes — after lunch."

"auto chat"

Assistant answers — marked as itself

It answers from the directory you opened it in — code, docs, git history — marked as your assistant, and writes what it learns back into that directory. What it can't ground, it asks you.

"auto chat read only" keeps the directory untouched · "stop" closes it any time

❯ auto chat, quiet

📨 Jonas · #1

is the export API rate-limited?

↳ 📤 sent as assistant — "Yes, 60 req/min per key."

📨 Jonas · #2

and what would enterprise pricing look like?

↳ ⚠️ needs you: Jonas is asking about enterprise pricing

…later: "handled 4 while you coded — 1 waiting on you"

## 02 · run it as

The front desk for your team.

No framework, no webhook, no deploy. Open auto chat in a repo and it answers from that directory — the code, the docs, the git history.

[auto chat]

Team bot

One desk the whole team writes with questions. It answers what the repo already knows, chases what it doesn't, and remembers every answer — an FAQ that writes itself.

→ the runbook

[read only]

Customer desk

Point it at your docs. Customers get grounded answers; the rest lands with you, whole exchange in hand. Invite-only, so no spam.

→ the runbook

[npx send]

Scripts & CI

Pipelines message you headlessly — npx cli-chat-mcp send ops "deploy landed" — and auto chat on your end closes the noise, answers the routine, and wakes you only when it matters.

→ the runbook

## 03 · how it works

Swap handles once. Message forever.

A 6-character handle is your phone number.

[2/3]

Log in with your email

## 04 · what you get

A messenger that already knows your context.

Portable, cross-CLI — and it sits where the answers already live.

[local-first]

Never waits on the network

History, contacts and recall are served from your device; sync catches up in the background.

[qL7f2A]

6-char handles

One short handle. New senders knock first — held until you say add. Rotate or disable it any time.

[magic-link]

Log in

Email login, any device — contacts and history included.

[mcp]

Cross-CLI

Claude Code to Gemini to Copilot — the wire doesn't care.

[history]

Messages are memory

"What did Niels say about the endpoint?" — recall any thread, any session.

[tty]

No new UI

No app. Your agent is the interface.
